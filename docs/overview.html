K9 Training · Phase one

What the site does

The website of K9 Training (آموزش K9), one dog trainer's private practice. He owns it and runs it as the Admin. Owners meet him on the site, book a free assessment for their dog, then buy private courses he teaches one dog at a time. Partners send customers through referral links and earn a commission. The site is in Persian, and everyone signs in with a phone number and an SMS code.

Private, one dog

At the owner's home or outside. Boarding is optional. No group classes, seats or venue.

Free first step

A new dog starts with a free assessment. The trainer then recommends courses.

Dogs only

Phase one is for dogs. Every course has an age range, sessions, a place and prerequisites.

Ready for more trainers

Every trainer has a public page and can submit courses for the Admin to approve.

Visitor

Anyone, before signing in.

Discover

  • Find a course by the dog's problem or by its age
  • Read each course: commands, sessions, place, price, refund policy
  • Read reviews and answered questions
  • See any trainer's page and courses

Start

  • Book the free assessment, or send a behavior request
  • Sign up with a phone number and a 5-digit code
  • Arrive through a partner's link or QR code, and stay that partner's customer

Customer

A dog owner. Account: signs up alone.

Dogs

  • Add dogs with breed, birth date, health and behavior notes
  • Each dog keeps a record: assessments, recommendations, results, passed tests

Book

  • Pick an open assessment slot, say what to change and where
  • Book a course or a level test; the site checks age and prerequisites
  • Pass a test instead of taking the course before it
  • Send a behavior request with a video; pay the trainer's plan

Pay

  • Pay by card, from the wallet, or both
  • Use discount codes
  • Cancel: full refund before the first session, then by the course's policy
  • Refunds land in the wallet; withdraw them to a bank account

Follow

  • See every session's time, place and the trainer's notes
  • Get SMS for bookings, reminders, results and refunds
  • Rate the course and the trainer, ask questions on course pages

Admin

The owner, who is also the head trainer. Account: one sign-in for both jobs.

Teach

  • Everything a trainer does, for his own dogs: slots, assessments, sessions, behavior plans
  • His public page is the one the home page features
  • His teaching income goes straight to the Admin wallet

People

  • Create trainers and partners, change roles, block users
  • Pick the head trainer, verify trainers

Catalog

  • Create his own courses directly; approve, reject or edit assistants' courses
  • Prices, ages, prerequisites, refund policy and trainer share are Admin-only after approval
  • Hand a course, or one dog's training, to an assistant

Money

  • See every order, payment, wallet and transaction
  • Fix mistakes with a new transaction or a mirror
  • Approve withdrawals after paying from the bank app
  • Discount codes and refund policies

Site

  • Moderate comments and questions
  • Change settings without a code release
  • Showcase mode: read every SMS the app would have sent

Trainer

His assistants, later other trainers. The owner uses these tools too. Account: made by the Admin.

Profile

  • A public page: photo, bio, years, specialties, certificates, rating
  • A verified badge, given by the Admin

Assess

  • Open assessment slots, one-off or repeating
  • Write notes and recommend courses after each assessment
  • Mark a dog as already known, so it can book directly
  • Review behavior requests: propose a plan and price, or decline

Teach

  • Schedule each session's time and place
  • Mark sessions held or missed, write notes
  • Record the result: passed or failed

Courses and pay

  • Create courses and submit them for approval (the owner's go live directly)
  • Reply to questions on their courses
  • See earnings per enrollment; withdraw from the wallet (the owner's share goes to the Admin wallet)

Partner

A pet shop, clinic or groomer. Account: made by the Admin.

Bring customers

  • Share a referral link or QR code
  • Register a customer's phone number before they sign up
  • A customer belongs to one partner forever

Earn

  • Get a commission on every purchase their customers make
  • See referred customers, their orders and spend
  • Withdraw the commission from the wallet

How money moves

Every user has a wallet. The app never sends money by itself.

Each sale is one transaction whose lines add up to zero. A refund is its mirror, every sign flipped. Users see only their balance and their own lines.
