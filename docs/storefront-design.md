# Storefront Design

The look, voice and patterns of the site, as the home page sets them. Since 2026-10-21 that is the mascot-led look the owner chose on `/new-design` (now the home page): a cartoon Belgian Malinois who talks to the visitor in chalk bubbles, on a near-black grained paper with bright gold buttons. Every page that is redesigned or rebuilt follows this doc; the mockups in `image-reference/` were the previous look's reference and still hold for photos. Pages still in the old look are listed in [legacy-pages.md](legacy-pages.md). For the steps to design a page, see [design-flow.md](design-flow.md); for code structure, [frontend-guideline.md](frontend-guideline.md).

The tokens are app-wide (`app/app.css`), so the signed-in areas use the same palette and faces: calmer and denser than the storefront, but the same paper, gold and voice. The Admin area takes only the tokens and its own shell: plain lists, no mascot story.

The shared `BrandLogo` keeps the original «آموزش K9» lettering and replaces only its dog mark with the owner-supplied gold K9 DOG emblem. Sharp exports in `public/brand/` preserve transparency and remove empty margins; the 64, 128 and 256px WebP versions serve different display densities. `app/root.tsx` declares the matching 16/32px PNG favicons, a 16/32/48px ICO and a 180px Apple touch icon on black.

## What the Site Must Feel Like

- **The dog talks to you.** The visitor is greeted by the mascot, not by a company: he lives in the layout (leaning over a wall, hanging from a band, running across a lane) and speaks in chalk bubbles, in informal Persian, about himself: «سلام، من زروانم! بیا تو، در بازه.». Headlines are in his voice too. See "Mascot" below.
- **It sells real people.** The dogs talk about their trainers by first name: the head trainer (`HEAD_TRAINER` in `app/lib/head-trainer.ts`) runs the evaluation, «اول بذار طلا منو بشناسه», and is the only trainer the home page introduces (the two placeholder colleagues' cards were removed with the old home page on 2026-10-21). Never an anonymous pool of trainers.
- **Drawn, then photographed.** The cartoon mascot carries the storefront's story; real photos are for real things (the head trainer, the customer's own dogs, course covers). Text and UI are quiet around both.
- **Warm, not a gold shop.** Near-black paper and bright, flat gold. Gold can go anywhere it helps: buttons, icons, numbers, names and headlines (the user lifted the old "gold only on buttons" rule on 2026-10-21).
- **Mostly private training.** The help page lists the owner's four formats (private at the client's place, private at the center, group at the center, boarding with coordination). Group classes aren't running yet: never word them as open, and never show seats or a class schedule.
- **Every road leads to the free evaluation.** It is the main action on every public page.

The header, footer and phone tab bar live in `app/routes/layout_public/` and wrap every public page:

- **Header** (`site-header.tsx`): on the paper over a hairline, the emblem and «زروان» with the descriptor, the four pages (دوره‌ها، درباره، تماس، راهنما), then «ورود» (or the signed-in person's cart and menu) and the gold «ارزیابی رایگان» pill. A magnifier opens the course search under the hairline (`header-search.tsx`): on `/courses` it is the list's live `q`, anywhere else Enter goes to `/courses?q=…`; it is a plain GET form, so it works before hydration. Below `lg` it is an app bar: the wordmark, the search and the account.
- **Tab bar** (`tab-bar.tsx`, below `lg`): a floating bar with خانه، دوره‌ها، the gold «ارزیابی» tile، تماس and «بیشتر», a bottom sheet (`more-sheet.tsx`) with the other pages (about, help, partnership, rules once approved), the account (sign in, or the person's area, settings and sign out) and the phone and social links. Anything else fixed to the bottom on phones sits above it (`--tab-bar-space`). A page can dock its own strip on top of the bar by portalling into `TAB_BAR_DOCK_ID` (`app/lib/tab-bar-dock.ts`): Course Detail's price and buy button once the buy card has scrolled away; while the strip is open (`data-dock-open`) the bar squares its top corners so the two read as one.
- **Footer** (`site-footer.tsx`): a grained `card` band with the brand and social links, three link groups, the phone, area and trainer, and a copyright line; its bottom padding clears the tab bar.

## Course Catalogue

`/courses` (`app/routes/course_catalogue/`) lists every course, grouped by family; its state is the URL (`?q=`, `?family=`, `?page=`), twelve cards to a page. The search has no field on the page: it is the header's magnifier (`HeaderSearch`), which on this page writes the same `q`, and the page's line points up at it.

- **The top:** the headline «همه‌ی چیزهایی که می‌تونم یاد بگیرم», one line and «خرید دوره» (a visitor is sent to sign in and back). Beside them the `peek` dog looks over a brick wall from a little garden (a bush each side, a pool of warm light), and his chalk bubble is the live result counter («۱۱ تا دوره دارم! یکی رو انتخاب کن.», «برای «cgc» یه دوره پیدا کردم.»), announced to screen readers. On load he climbs up from behind the wall, then breathes; the bubble pops from its tail.
- **The shelf:** the wall itself, a full-bleed brick band (`BRICK` under the grain, a light coping on top where his paws rest) holding the family pills (`CourseFamilyFilter`); on phones they scroll sideways.
- **The families:** each family on the page is one sunken, grained panel with its header and its cards, so no grid cell is left empty. The header is the family's dog (`familyMascotPose`) on a short `GroundStrip` path in front of the family's dimmed scenery (`course-scenes`), with the family name and one plain line. One course: the header is a stage beside the card from tablets up, with his bubble in a night sky; two or four: a stage beside a two-column block on laptops; three, or five and more: a row across the top. Two one-course families share a row on laptops; a lone one gets a bigger dog.
- **The answers:** a new list (family, search or page) fades in panel by panel (`answer-in`, staggered), and only the counter's words change in his bubble. Nothing found: the bubble says so, and a card offers «همه‌ی دوره‌ها» and the free evaluation.
- **The end:** a grained card with no dog asks «نمی‌دونی از کدوم شروع کنیم؟» and sends to the free evaluation.

## Login / Register

`/auth` (`app/routes/auth/`) sits outside the storefront shell. Since 2026-10-21 it is the mascot's front door instead of a photo per step: one half is a night stage with the `door` in its stretch of brick wall, its lamp lit, a path, bushes and the step's dog waiting on the doorstep with his chalk bubble asking the step's question (the `wave` dog for the number, `key-carry` for the password, `smartphone` for the SMS code, `listen` for the name). The other half holds the logo, «وارد شو، منتظرتم» with «منتظرتم» in gold, and the step's form on one grained plate, with the demo accounts (showcase mode) inside it and the trust note under it. On phones the stage is an 18rem band on top. Motion: on load the lamp lights, the dog trots up and his bubble pops; on a step change the old dog steps down, the new one hops in, the bubble pops again and the form rises in; errors rise in too. The flow, its ids and focus rules are unchanged.

## Help and FAQ

`/help` (`app/routes/help/`) answers how buying works without a phone call. The idea: the five-stop chalk road under the header is the overview of the journey (free evaluation, courses open, pay, call and training, done) and also the index into the FAQ below, whose six groups carry the same numbered discs (the last group, «ورود و شماره‌ات», is not a step and has none). A group's disc fills gold when it is the `:target`. The header's thinking dog stands in front of a notice board («سؤال داری؟ از اینجا شروع کن!»). The road is a chalk board drawn like the home trail, with the signpost at the start, the key at stop 2 and the gold flag at stop 5; the dog on the phone sits on its top edge above stop 4 with the page's key fact («بعد از ثبت درخواستت، طلا باهات تماس می‌گیره.»). Each group's heading is a small stage with the dog of that step (the notebook, the key, the basket, the leash, the medal and the flag, a wave for the account group), sticky beside its accordion on desktop; under a short accordion the stage's path runs on and fades. Answers are plain body copy, not the dog's voice: the dog speaks in the headings and the bubbles. The closing ask keeps the phone and Telegram, with the letter dog on its edge («یا یه پیام بفرست، می‌رسونمش!»). Question ids are the anchors other pages link to (`how-buying-works`, `after-paying`, `payment-failed`, …); group ids are `evaluation`, `courses`, `paying`, `training`, `done`, `account`. The copy never states refund or cancellation rules, deadlines, expiry, a calendar or lesson tracking: the one money-back sentence is that {طلا} cancels and returns the money outside the site if he can't take the dog. Motion: the header bubble pops on load, and the dog of the group the road (or another page's link) leads to hops once as its disc fills, pure CSS on `:target`.

## Privacy

`/privacy` (`app/routes/privacy/`) was moved into the mascot look on 2026-10-21: the dog guards your things. The header's `guard` dog sits on duty by a locked `chest` («ببین چی رو نگه می‌دارن؛ همه‌ش اینجا نوشته.»). On desktop a sticky rail on a grained plate lists the sections, with the `watch` dog lying along its top edge, the photo note under it and the `door` with the sleeping dog closing the column. «چی ذخیره می‌شه» is a definition list on a grained card with the dimmed `cabinet` on its edge. «کی چی رو می‌بینه» is the page's one brick wall: three dark plates hang on it as signs of their own lengths, each with its viewer's little scene (you: the `sit` dog by the `home`; the head trainer: the notebook dog by the `center`; a partner: the `shopping` dog by the `petshop`), and the partner's plate also lists what a partner does not see. «چی رو خودت عوض می‌کنی» has the `key-carry` dog, the collapsed «جزئیات فنی» is a native `<details>` whose rows rise in when it opens, and the closing card has the `think` dog by the mailbox («منم اولش کلی سؤال داشتم!»). Motion: the bubble pops and the chest settles on load, the plates reveal on scroll, and a section's dog hops once when it is the `:target`. The page says it in plain words (the owner found the first version too technical, 2026-10-19): what is kept, who sees what, what you change yourself, and the details that keep every fact the first version disclosed (IP address and browser, the wrong-password record, the code limits, the SMS log, the partner cookie, the cart copy in the browser). The partner plate matches what the partner's leads page really shows (the customer's name and number, where they stand, the commission and whether it is paid; never a dog, an evaluation, the diary, a purchase or its price). **It is a placeholder:** it states only what the app does today and makes no claim about retention periods, deletion rights, third parties or "we never share". It carries no "last updated" date.

## Partner Enquiry

`/join/partner` (`app/routes/join_partner/`) is for the owner of a clinic, groomer or pet shop, not a customer, so its main action is the phone call, not the free evaluation. **It has no form**: the API has no way to receive an enquiry and the Admin has no screen to read one yet (his Partners page is built, but the enquiry table and procedure are not). Moved into the mascot look on 2026-10-21: the hero is a little street, the `petshop` and the `clinic` with the `groomed` dog (the groomer's customer) in front, his bubble addressed to the shop owner («یه سگ مثل من پیش تو میاد؟ بفرستش پیش طلا.»). The three ways in (link, QR, phone leads) hang as plates on the page's one brick wall, with the shop `counter` and the `present` dog on its coping («من از همین پیشخون راه افتادم!»). The three steps (call, the Admin creates the account, sign in) are walked on a chalk board's path from the signpost to the gold flag, a dog at each step (on the phone, with the key, jumping for joy); beside them the commission plate shows the percent big in gold under the `basket` dog, and the closing card asks for the call with the waving dog by the mailbox («منتظر زنگتم!»). Motion: the street rises and the bubble pops on load, the plates reveal, the path draws itself and the step dogs pop in on scroll. The commission percent shown is the setting in force (`setting.commissionPercent`), read by the page's loader.

## About

`/about` (`app/routes/about/`) introduces the head trainer through the dog, built only from what the owner printed on the business card (name, role, 13 years since 1392, the international credential, the Tehran service area): no biography and no claim about where or how training is held. It is the one place a visitor reads about the trainer (the nav has no «مربی»). The hero is the page's one brick wall: the owner's standing photo taped to it as a crooked print, and a plate with her name in gold, the one sentence, the free evaluation and the courses; the waving dog sits on the coping by a hedge («سلام! اینم طلاست، مربی من.»). The three facts each get a small scene in one sunken panel: the year with the notebook dog at the center, the credential with the diploma dog and the flag, the area with the leash dog in front of the Tehran skyline, cropped to the Milad tower. «پیش طلا اینا رو یاد می‌گیرم» lines up the family dogs on one path, each a link to `/courses?family=`, ending at a signpost to all courses; the loader reads `course.list` for the families with courses, and if it fails the lineup is left out. On phones the lineup is a two-column grid whose last cell never leaves a hole. The closing card asks for a call or a message, with the sitting dog, a bench and a lamp on its edge («من اولش فقط یه توله‌ی شیطون بودم!»). Motion: on load the print drops onto the wall from its tape and swings to rest, then the waving dog's bubble pops; the lineup's dogs lift on hover and keyboard focus; the facts rise in on scroll.

## Dog Record

`/logbook` and `/logbook/:fileNumber` (`app/routes/logbook/`) are what a dog's QR code opens, and the lookup that types its number. Moved into the mascot look on 2026-10-21: the record belongs to the dog. **The lookup:** «پرونده‌ی سگ» («سگ» in gold), the line that the number is on the dog's QR code, and the form on a grained plate (the LTR mono field, «باز کن»), beside the `tag` dog showing the tag on his collar in front of the dimmed `kennel` («شماره‌ی پرونده‌م رو QR‌مه؛ تو هم پیداش می‌کنی.»). **A private record** (the same page, word for word and picture for picture, for a real, a made-up or a malformed number): a gold lock disc beside «این پرونده خصوصیه», the two lines, «ورود» for a visitor and «یه شماره‌ی دیگه بنویس», beside the `folder` dog hugging the closed folder in front of the padlocked `gate` («ببخشید! این پرونده رو فقط صاحبش می‌بینه.»). Both end on the page's one brick wall (`flushFooter`), «پرونده‌ی هر سگ پیش کیه؟»: three plates (the owner, who is sent to the dog's page; the head trainer, who writes in it after each session; everyone else, who sees only that it is private) with the `peek` dog over the coping. **A public record** (switched off until the owner approves the consent wording) is presented by the mascot as his friend's, never as the dog itself («این پرونده‌ی رفیقم {name}ه!»): the name in gold, breed and age, the finished courses as medal cards, the valid certificates as rows linking to their check, and the public diary entries on a chalk timeline. Motion: the lookup's plate rises in and the tag dog hops; a private record's gate rattles on its latch and the lock disc shakes; a malformed number shakes the field and its error rises in (and folds away as you type); «باز کن» turns into a spinner while the record loads; the wall's plates reveal and the peek dog rises over the coping on scroll. Never cached and never indexed.

## Certificate Check

`/certificate/:number` (`app/routes/certificate/`) is what a certificate's QR code opens, usually on a stranger's phone, so the verdict comes first (on a phone the h1 sits right under a 13.5rem stage band). Moved into the mascot look on 2026-10-21: the dog checks it for you. **Valid:** a gold check disc and «این گواهی معتبره» («معتبره» in gold), then a grained result card with a thin gold inner frame and the round `seal-disc` stamped over its top end corner; the facts are the course (and its Latin name), the dog's name only while its record is public, the day, the head trainer (a link to `/about`) and the number in gold; the stage is the dimmed `easel` with the `magnify` dog («با ذره‌بین نگاش کردم؛ همه‌چی درسته!»). **Revoked:** a red disc, «این گواهی باطل شده» and only the number and day on a muted card, with the `sorry` dog («ببخشید، این یکی دیگه اعتبار نداره.»). **Not found** (the same for text that isn't a number): «گواهی‌ای با این شماره پیدا نشد», a field to type the number again (uppercased, `ZC-` added when missing), and the `sniff` dog on the path to the dug `hole` («همه‌جا رو بو کشیدم، پیداش نکردم.»). Every state ends on a card asking for a call, with the dog on the phone by the mailbox on its top edge («سؤالی داشتی، زنگ بزن!»). The scenes put the scenery on the right and the dog on the left in physical terms, because every dog faces right. Motion: valid, the card rises and the seal stamps down onto it, then the bubble pops; revoked, the dog sinks a little; not found, the dog walks in along the path; a retry swaps the hero in with `answer-in`. Never cached (a cached «valid» would outlive a revocation) and never indexed.

## Rules and Not Found

**Not Found** (`app/components/not-found-page.tsx`) answers every address nothing else does, through a splat route under the public layout (`app/routes/not_found/`), and `/rules` while it is unpublished; before 2026-10-21 both fell through to the root's bare English «404». It keeps the 404 status and sits in the shell. The dog went looking and dug: a big gold «۴۰۴» (decoration; the h1 is «این صفحه اینجا نیست»), «شاید آدرسش عوض شده یا یه حرفش اشتباه تایپ شده.» and three ways on (صفحه‌ی اول, دوره‌ها, راهنما), beside the `dig` dog at the dug `hole` with its bone and a signpost pointing back at it («همه‌جا رو کندم؛ فقط یه استخون پیدا کردم!»). Setting `SITE_RULES` is all it takes to publish it.

## Evaluation Request

`/assessment` (`app/routes/assessment/`), in the public shell (it is the tab bar's «ارزیابی»), customers only. Moved into the mascot look on 2026-10-21: **the center's dog takes your request to {trainer}.** The header («رایگانه», «ارزیابی رایگان») carries a four-stop road (معرفی سگ، درخواست، زنگ {trainer}، دوره‌ها باز می‌شن) lit at where the customer is. The form is three numbered plates joined by a chalk line: «کدوم سگ؟» (each dog as a small taped print; a dog that can't ask says why; the picked dog answers «آماده‌م!»), «کی وقت داری؟» and the optional «عکس و فیلم» (a dashed chalk drop zone, thumbnails as prints). ## Admin Pages

The Admin area (`/admin`, `app/routes/layout_admin/`) has **the site's style, not its story** (the user, 2026-10-21): the grained paper, Estedad headings and big numbers, grained plates (`ADMIN_PLATE` in `app/lib/admin-plate.ts`) with ink hairlines between rows, and the slips' dashed tear (`ADMIN_TEAR`), but no mascot, bubbles, scenery, brick, idle loops or load moments. It stays dense and work-first: `text-sm` rows, targets of at least 40px (44px for a phone's main buttons), one-click buttons, informal copy. Gold marks only work that waits for him (a count above zero), the selected filter, one main button per row or dialog and the rail's current page; amounts and counts are ivory. Motion only answers him: a list that changed under a filter rises in (`answer-in`), a count that changed pops (`answer-pop`, keyed by value, never on load), a row that changed state is stamped, and panels open; reduced motion shows the finished state. The shell is the account header's frame (the emblem, «زروان», a gold-outlined «مدیریت» chip on every width, «دیدن سایت» and the person menu) on the paper over an ink hairline; a rail at the start side on desktop, its two groups split by a dashed tear and the current page on a raised pill whose gold notch grows in after a navigation; the account shell's floating tab bar on phones, with the «بیشتر» sheet. Three tabs carry the work waiting for him, from one `dashboard.admin.overview` call in the layout: Evaluations (requests), Payments (paid, not enrolled) and Partners (unpaid commissions). The shared Admin pieces are `AdminPageHeader` (the Estedad h1 and one summary line), `AdminFilterPills` (the count in a disc that pops when it changes), `AdminEmptyState` (a dashed plate with a line icon; no dog) and `CustomerContact` (its `call` variant a gold-outlined pill). Every Admin list loads its whole list once and keeps its filter, page and open drawer as shallow nuqs params; statuses only move forward, so every write goes through `useAdminAction`, which refreshes the list when the row changed under him.

- Payments (`/admin/payments`): the customer's slip made into a ledger. A paid payment is a slip: the stub (date, status, the total in Estedad, the deposit note, the customer to call, their free times, the gateway's reference and card) torn from its lines by a dashed line with notches, then the refunds under a double rule. The idea is the **torn piece**: a paid item with no enrollment gets a jagged edge and a dashed destructive outline, never worded as a debt, and a flag plate at the top counts them. Failed and pending payments collapse to one thin stub row. A filter change makes the slips rise in, a recorded refund rises in as «قابل بازپرداخت» pops, a refund marked paid or cancelled has its chip stamped, and the flag folds away when the last flagged item is resolved.
- Enrollments (`/admin/enrollments`): a call sheet. **One block per customer**, its stub a call card (the name in Estedad, the number as a big gold-outlined call pill, how many enrollments and dogs) torn from the lines; each active line has «تمام شد» (gold) and «لغو». The idea is that **a closed training is stamped and leaves**: after «تمام شد» a stamp lands where the buttons were and the line folds away; «لغو» dims the line and strikes the course through, then folds; a block whose last line left folds as a whole. «لغو» says in its dialog that money goes back only as a refund recorded on Payments.
- Evaluations (`/admin/evaluations`): a contact sheet. The idea is that each row carries up to three of the customer's photos as a strip of overlapping prints (a «+N» disc, a video chip, or «عکسی نفرستاده»), so the Admin skims the queue by faces and opens a request only to call. The rows sit in one plate, grouped under muted day labels when the list spans more than one day; a waiting row has a short gold notch on its start edge, and in «در انتظار» its chip isn't drawn (the filter already says it). An answered request is **stamped and leaves**: «ارزیابی شد» or «رد شد» lands where he pressed, then the row folds away (a day whose last row left folds with its label). The drawer is a `Sheet` because the `Drawer` component positions physically and is wrong in RTL: the head with the dog's name in Estedad (a link to Dog Detail), the call pill, «کی آزاده؟», the photo prints with a lightbox, the videos, and a footer with the answers or the outcome in his words. The open request is `?evaluation=<id>` (the new-request SMS links to it), and it stays open on the new status after an answer, its chip stamped.
- Dashboard (`/admin`): an overview where **only the work is lit**. One slip-shaped plate at the top, «منتظر توست», holds the day's work: the evaluation requests (a gold Estedad number in a gold ring above zero) and the paid-not-enrolled items (a torn piece with a jagged edge), torn from «آخرین خریدها» by a dashed line; a good day folds both into one calm sentence, so the page shows no gold at all. The summary line starts with today's Jalali date (computed in the loader on the Tehran clock). Below, in calm ivory: «ثبت‌نام‌ها» (three numbers over one proportional strip), «پول» (this month's takings big, the total, unpaid commissions; refunds are recorded on Payments and not subtracted) and «دوره‌ها» (a thin bar of active and finished per course, unbought ones muted). A number that changes after a revalidation pops and the bars slide to their new share; nothing moves on load.
- Dogs (`/admin/dogs`): a register read by faces and stamps. One plate of rows with a muted column head from `xl`; each row starts with the dog's print (a dashed square with the initial when there is no photo) and ends with one pill (`DogEvaluationStamp`, all the same width so they form a column), so he can run down the edge and know who is where on the road. The whole row is the link (the name carries a stretched link; the owner's number sits above it). Only the selected pill and «منتظر ارزیابی» are gold. A filter, search or page change makes the rows rise in and the result count pop.
- Dog Detail (`/admin/dogs/:dogId`): the ladder. Courses are rungs in catalogue order on one plate with an ink rail, because courses open one after another: a passed rung is a filled **ivory** disc with a dark check (with an enrollment or by hand), an open one a **gold ring** (the only gold on the ladder: where the dog can go next), a locked one an ink lock with the reason that names the rung to climb first. - Courses (`/admin/courses`): the shelf in the order a dog climbs it. One plate of rows with a muted column head; each row's position is a big Estedad number with its two arrows, so the order is something he can touch (courses open one after another), and the «بعد از:» lines show which rung opens which. **Moving a course moves it**: the two rows slide past each other (the moved one lifted) and both numbers pop; a failed move slides them back. An archived course stays on the shelf, dashed and dimmed, with a stamped «بایگانی‌شده» chip and «برگرداندن». Nothing on the page is gold; «دوره‌ی تازه» is an outline button.
- Course Form (`/admin/courses/new`, `/admin/courses/:slug/edit`): the card as the customer will see it. The real `CourseCard` sits beside the form (under it on phones) and updates as he types, so the preview is true by construction.
- Platform Settings (`/admin/settings`): each number says what it will do, live: one sentence under the input is recomputed as he types, with a concrete example.
- SMS Outbox (`/admin/sms`): messages look like the SMS on the receiver's phone (a `bg-muted` bubble, never the dog's chalk bubble), newest first, grouped by kind pills.

## Colour and Surfaces

The tokens are in `app/app.css` ("Near-black Paper & Bright Gold"). One dark theme; there is no light mode.

- **Paper:** the page is a neutral near-black, `#141414` (`--base`, `background`, `jet`), with a faint grain (`--grain`, an SVG noise on the `body`; `paper-grain` puts it on a band that paints its own fill). Raised surfaces step up in neutral greys: `card` `#1D1D1D`, `popover` and `inverse` `#232323`, `muted` `#2B2B2B`; `sunken` `#0F0F0F` is a step down. Never warm or tinted: under gold, a warm black reads as mud.
- **Gold:** `primary` `#FFC21A`, with near-black `primary-foreground` `#1A1300` (11:1) and `primary-hover` `#FFD14F`. For buttons, icons, step numbers, the selected filter, accents, and headlines, names or big numbers where a page wants them. `border` is a faint gold hairline; the mascot pages draw their own hairlines in ink (`border-foreground/15`). `gold-leaf`, `text-gold-leaf` and `saffron` are the same flat gold.
- **Text:** ivory ink (`foreground` `#F4EEE2`); `muted-foreground` `#A8A297` for secondary text (5.6:1 even on `muted`). The `ChalkBubble` is drawn in the ink; **chalk** (`text-chalk`, white) is the doodles'.
- **Layers, back to front:** `sunken`, the paper, `card` + `shadow-sm` (raised), `popover` + `shadow-lg` (overlays). One grey band per page at most (`surface-inverse`, which re-points the paper inside it, so a bubble there is cut from the band).
- **The dog is the brightest thing.** Scenery cut-outs (plants, ground, buildings) are dimmed with `scenery-dim`; the dog and his gold props are not.

## Type

- **Three faces, three jobs.**
  - `font-display`: **Estedad** at 800 (`DISPLAY_HEADING` in `app/lib/display-heading.ts`: tight leading, ivory). The hero headline and section `<h2>`s only.
  - `font-sans`: **Vazirmatn**, for everything else, including `<h3>`s inside a section.
  - `font-hand`: **Marhey**, only inside speech bubbles (the dog's voice). Never for UI or body text. The font file is patched for the medial «ی» dots (see `app/app.css`).
- The wordmark «زروان» is Estedad at 900 beside the emblem. The stencil face (`font-stencil`) is only for the «K9» on the dog tag.
- **Sizes:** the home page sets its headings per scene (`text-4xl` to `md:text-6xl`); the fluid `text-display`, `text-h1` and `text-lead` remain for the other pages. Body, buttons and inputs stay on Tailwind's static scale.
- **Persian needs room:** tall line heights, never negative letter spacing on body text, `text-balance` on headings, `text-pretty` on paragraphs.
- **Numbers** in Persian digits (`formatNumberFa`, `toPersianDigits`). Latin names and phone numbers in `dir="ltr"` or `<bdi>`.
- No single word in a headline coloured differently (the hero's gold chalk underline under the trainer's name is a drawing, not colour), and no small labels above section titles.

## Actions

- **Buttons are pills** (the base style is `rounded-full`). `default` is flat gold, the main action; `outline` is the quieter alternative. `size="lg"` for page-level actions, `size="sm"` inside cards.
- **One main action per view, one quieter alternative.** Never two equal buttons side by side.
- **Button wording is plain**, even though the page speaks as the dog: «ارزیابی رایگان بگیر», «دوره‌ها را ببین», «مشاهده دوره». It says what happens.

## Voice and Copy

- **The dog speaks** in headlines and bubbles: first person, informal spoken Persian (`می‌شه`, `منو`, `خونه‌مون`), short, warm, a little playful: «من فقط فرمان نمی‌برم؛ می‌فهمم.», «چی می‌تونم یاد بگیرم؟». Supporting paragraphs can stay in the dog's voice; course data (titles, summaries) stays as the catalogue writes it.
- **Facts only.** The dog describes how training here works (the kinds of class the help page words, free evaluation first, then the course and the call), never promises results it can't back. «Private» and «at home or outside» are not promises: the kind of class is arranged with the trainer, and where a course is held is on its own page (the owner's notes, 2026-10-19).
- Course facts are approximate and say so: use `describeSessions`, `describeAge`, `describePlace` and `formatTomanShort` from `app/lib/course-format.ts`.
- Never write that every dog should take CGC or almost every dog BMC.
- The owner’s supplied business card includes behavior modification and consultation before and after training. Describe them as services; they do not add purchasable course families or change the evaluation and checkout flow.

## Layout

- Content sits in `site-container`; photo bands go full width and put their content back in one.
- Sections are separated by `pt-section-lg` on the home page. Anchored sections carry `scroll-mt-24`.
- Desktop splits are asymmetric (7/5, 5/7), not centred halves.
- **Desktop and phone are designed separately.** The home page's examples: the hero's photo goes on top on phones with the words below; the cut-out dog sits on the card's top edge on phones and stands at its end on desktop; the bubble's tail points up at the dog on phones and across at it on desktop.
- Radii: `rounded-3xl` for cards and photos, `rounded-4xl` for a big panel, `rounded-full` for buttons and pills.

## Motion

- **Idle loops** keep the mascot alive: the shared `lively-*` classes in `app/app.css` (`lively-wiggle`, `-tilt`, `-hop`, `-swing`, `-breathe`, `-twitch` on the dog; `lively-sway` on plants; `lively-float` on bubbles; `lively-pet` wriggles him on hover; `lively-lift` and `lively-lift-pic` for a card; `lively-reveal` and `lively-reveal-pop` tie a reveal to the scroll where the browser can). Long and small, so the page feels alive without nagging. They animate `transform`, which composes with Tailwind's `translate`/`rotate`/`scale`.
- **Answers** follow what the visitor does: `answer-in` (new content rises a few pixels as it fades in; `--answer-delay` staggers a list) and `answer-pop` (a bubble pops from its tail, `--pop-origin`), in `app/app.css`. They run on mount, so a changed part gets a new `key`. The courses pages use them for the filtered list, the counter, and the picked dog's answer on the buy card.
- **One orchestrated moment per page at most.** The home page's is the hero: the dog rises from behind the wall, the bubble pops, the headline types and the underline draws; his bark is the visitor's to trigger. A page's one-off motion stays in its slice (the hero's, the runner's lap).
- Motion is added **after** the still page has been reviewed (see "Mascot"). No new motion library: `motion` and `lenis` are already here.
- Only `transform` and `opacity`. Everything is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`, which shows the finished page.

- **How a page gets him** (agreed with the user, 2026-10-21):
  1. The brief plans where he sits on the page, what he leans on or peeks over, and what he says, so the layout is built around him.
  2. The page is built still, with the poses in place and no animation.
  3. Screenshots go to the user, who moves or drops the dog where he doesn't work.
  4. Only then is the motion added, from the shared idle loops and the home page's reveal code.
- Working pages (the catalogue, Course Detail, the account) keep him calmer than the home page: no running laps or extra gimmicks, but not bare. The approved courses pages give every dog an edge to stand on, scenery, warm light and texture, and Course Detail has him in four places. The Admin area has no mascot story.
- **How to get there without three review rounds:** [mascot-page-playbook.md](mascot-page-playbook.md), what the courses redesign taught: the patterns, the cut-outs' edges, the motion and the pitfalls.
