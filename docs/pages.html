Visitor

Anyone, no account. Booking forms ask them to sign in.

  1. 1HomeBUILT/

    Meet the head trainer, find a course by problem or by the dog's age, book the free assessment.

  2. 2Course Catalogue/courses

    Browse every course by family. Filter by family, dog's age and place; search.

  3. 3Course DetailNEXT/courses/:slug

    Read commands, age, sessions, place, prerequisites, price, refund policy, reviews and questions. Book an assessment, the course, its test, or send a behavior request.

  4. 4Trainer Page/trainers/:slug

    See a trainer's bio, years, specialties, certificates, courses, rating and reviews.

  5. 5Book a Free AssessmentNEXT/assessment

    Pick or add a dog, say what should change and where to meet, choose an open slot.

  6. 6Behavior Request/behavior-modification

    Pick the problem, describe it, upload a video of it.

  7. 7Login / RegisterBUILT/auth

    Enter a phone number, then the 5-digit SMS code.

  8. 8Help and FAQ/help

    Read answers to common questions.

  9. 9Refund Rules/help/refunds

    Read the cancellation and refund rules in plain words.

  10. 10Privacy/privacy

    Read what is stored and why.

  11. 11Partner Enquiry/join/partner

    Shops, clinics and groomers learn about the referral program and call the center.

Customer

A dog owner, signed in.

  1. 12Dashboard

    See upcoming sessions and assessments and what each dog needs next.

  2. 13Dogs List

    See all their dogs. Replaces the old /pets demo.

  3. 14Dog Form

    Add or edit a dog: breed, birth date, weight, vaccinations, behavior and medical notes (shown to the trainer).

  4. 15Dog Detail and Record

    See assessments, recommendations, courses, results and passed tests. Book the next course from here.

  5. 16Book a Course or Test

    Choose the dog, boarding, address, preferred times and goal; see eligibility notices.

  6. 17Checkout

    Review items, apply a discount code, use wallet balance, pay.

  7. 18Payment Result

    See whether payment worked; retry if not.

  8. 19My Training

    See every course their dogs are taking or took, by status.

  9. 20Enrollment Detail

    See sessions, places, notes and the result; cancel with a refund preview.

  10. 21My Assessments

    See upcoming and past assessments; cancel an upcoming one.

  11. 22My Behavior Requests

    Follow each request; pay the trainer's plan before it expires, or withdraw.

  12. 23My Orders

    See past orders and payments.

  13. 24Write Review

    Rate the course and the trainer from 1 to 5 and leave a comment.

Shared

Customers, assistants and partners.

  1. 25Profile Settings

    Edit their name and details.

  2. 26Wallet

    See balance, pending withdrawal and their own transaction lines.

  3. 27Withdrawal Request

    Ask to be paid to a Sheba number (up to 72 hours); cancel a pending request.

Trainer

Assistants, and the owner for his own teaching. My Courses, Course Form and Review Changes are for assistants only.

  1. 28Dashboard

    Today's sessions and assessments, courses waiting to be scheduled, new behavior requests.

  2. 29Schedule

    Week view. Open and close assessment slots; see overlap warnings.

  3. 30Assessment Detail

    Mark done or no-show, write notes, recommend courses, mark a dog as already known.

  4. 31Enrollments

    See the courses they're teaching, by status.

  5. 32Enrollment Detail

    Schedule sessions, mark held or missed, write notes, change the count, record pass or fail.

  6. 33Behavior Requests

    See incoming requests.

  7. 34Behavior Request Detail

    Watch the videos; propose sessions and a price, or decline.

  8. 35My Courses

    See their courses and each one's approval status.

  9. 36Course Form

    Create, edit or resubmit a course; see why it was rejected.

  10. 37Review Changes

    See the Admin's edits field by field; accept or edit them.

  11. 38Comments to Reply

    Answer questions on their courses.

  12. 39Earnings

    See what they earned per enrollment.

Partner

A pet shop, clinic or groomer that refers customers.

  1. 40Dashboard

    Customers brought in and commission at a glance.

  2. 41Referral Link and QR

    Copy the link, save the QR code.

  3. 42Referred Customers

    See signup date, paid orders and spend; filter by date.

  4. 43Leads

    Register a customer's phone number in advance; see if they signed up.

Admin

The owner, who is also the head trainer. Sees and manages everything.

  1. 44Dashboard

    What needs attention: courses to approve, withdrawals, negative balances.

  2. 45Users

    Create trainers and partners, change roles, block, pick the head trainer, verify trainers.

  3. 46User Detail / Form

    See and edit one user.

  4. 47Courses

    See every course by status.

  5. 48Course Review / Form

    Create a course; approve, reject, edit and send back, or force approve. Archive, change owner.

  6. 49Assessments

    See every assessment.

  7. 50Enrollments

    See every enrollment.

  8. 51Enrollment Detail

    Hand it to another trainer; cancel with a reason and refund.

  9. 52Behavior Requests

    See every request and its plan.

  10. 53Orders

    See every order.

  11. 54Order Detail

    See one order's items, payment and transactions.

  12. 55Payments

    Read the card payment log. Nobody can edit it.

  13. 56Transactions

    See every transaction; warned before mirroring one twice.

  14. 57Transaction Form

    Move money between any wallets, or start from the mirror of a transaction.

  15. 58Wallets

    See system and user wallets and every negative balance.

  16. 59Withdrawal Queue

    Oldest first, over 72 hours highlighted. Approve with a tracking code or decline.

  17. 60Discount Codes

    See every code and its use.

  18. 61Discount Code Form

    Create or edit a code and its limits.

  19. 62Refund Policies

    Set how much of the unheld sessions comes back.

  20. 63Comments Moderation

    Approve, reject, hide, restore or reply.

  21. 64Partners and Leads

    See partners, their leads and commission.

  22. 65Platform Settings

    Change app-wide values, such as the default trainer share and slot length.

  23. 66SMS Outbox

    Showcase only: read every SMS the app would have sent.

Built pages: Home and Login. Next: Course Detail and Book a Free Assessment, the two pages most home page buttons lead to.
